PaceTick Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026

This policy explains what data the PaceTick app for iPhone ("the app") handles, why, and the choices you have. PaceTick is a running metronome. It has no accounts, and you never need to sign in.

  • Your cadence (SPM), volume, sound, height, pace and unit settings stay on your iPhone. We never receive them.
  • The app uses Google services to show ads (AdMob), measure how the app is used (Google Analytics for Firebase), and report crashes (Firebase Crashlytics).
  • Ads are personalized only if you allow it. You can say no to tracking and the app works the same.

1. Data kept only on your device

The app saves your last cadence, click volume, click sound, the height and pace you entered in the cadence calculator, and your distance and height units, so they are there the next time you open it. This data is stored only on your iPhone. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and it is deleted when you delete the app.

The app does not ask for your location, microphone, contacts, photos, or health data.

The app includes software from Google LLC. When you use the app with an internet connection, these services collect the data below and send it to Google's servers.

3. Tracking and ad personalization

  • App Tracking Transparency: The app asks whether it may track your activity across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the advertising identifier is not shared, and you still see ads that are not based on tracking. You can change this any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland: If you are in a region where consent is required, the app shows Google's consent form before personalized ads are used. You can change your choice later in the app under Settings > Ad Privacy Settings.

4. How long data is kept

Data on your device is kept until you delete the app. Data collected by Google is kept according to Google's settings for each service: usage data linked to an app instance in Google Analytics is kept for 2 months, and Crashlytics crash reports are kept for 90 days. AdMob data is kept as described in Google's policies.

5. International transfer

Google may process the data in section 2 in the United States and other countries where Google operates. Google uses safeguards such as standard contractual clauses for these transfers.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Turn off tracking in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Change ad consent in the app (Settings > Ad Privacy Settings), where it applies.
  • Delete the app to remove all data stored on your device.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of your data. Contact us at the address below and we will help, including asking Google to delete analytics data linked to your app instance.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

8. Security

Data sent to Google services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Settings kept on your device are protected by iOS.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. For significant changes, we will also let you know in the app or in the App Store release notes.
